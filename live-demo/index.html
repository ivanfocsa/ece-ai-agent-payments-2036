03 / WHO IS AFFECTED?

The stakeholders have different priorities.

USERS

Convenience, consent, privacy, refunds and control.

AI PROVIDERS

Accurate agents, safeguards, transparency and responsibility.

MERCHANTS

Simple transactions, verified service delivery, fair access.

WALLET PROVIDERS

Secure credentials, fraud controls and clear receipts.

FRENCH AUTHORITIES

Consumer protection, financial compliance and innovation.

SOCIETY

Fair access, job changes, trust and responsible automation.

04 / ETHICAL BATTLE

PROTECTION FIRST OR INNOVATION FIRST?

PROTECTION FIRST

High-risk payments should require informed approval, merchant checks and a right to challenge mistakes.

INNOVATION FIRST

Too many approval steps might destroy the advantages of faster, cheaper machine-to-machine services.

FIND THE BALANCE

Small trusted transactions can be automatic; risky purchases should stop for review.

Use utilitarianism, duty-based ethics and individual rights to defend your choice.

05 / A POLICY CHALLENGE FOR FRANCE

THREE RULES TO DISCUSS.

PERMISSION BY DESIGN

Every wallet needs visible budgets, allowed actions and an emergency stop.

VERIFIED PAYMENTS

Use proportionate merchant checks, risk signals and human approval.

ACCOUNTABILITY

Provide payment explanations, accessible records and clear dispute procedures.

These are academic proposals for 2036, not current French laws or official ministry recommendations.
