# Three social media posts — France 2036

These are **academic mockups**, not genuine government posts and not posted to any social network.

## Post 1 — Hook (Instagram)
**Question:** Would you give an AI access to €100?

**Caption:**
> Imagine your AI assistant needs to buy a digital service for €35. Would you let it pay automatically, ask you first, or reject it?
>
> Vote YES / NO / DEPENDS — tell us why.
>
> #France2036 #AIAgents #AIethics #YourMoney

**In class:** ask for a quick show of hands.

## Post 2 — Explain (LinkedIn / informational carousel)
**Question:** How can a website ask an AI to pay?

**Caption:**
> Protocols such as x402 can let services request small payments from software. By 2036, we imagine wallets that check a user's budget, consent and the trustworthiness of sellers before paying.
>
> Fast payments are useful. Clear rules are important too.
>
> #x402 #France2036 #DigitalEconomy #ResponsibleAI

**Diagram:** Request → HTTP 402 payment request → agent checks policy → human approval if necessary → simulated access.

**Research:** Reppel, E., Dalal, N., & Kim, D. (2025). Introducing x402. Coinbase.

## Post 3 — Debate (Instagram or LinkedIn)
**Question:** Who pays when the AI makes a mistake?

**Caption:**
> Your AI spends €35 at an unknown online service. The money is gone and the service never arrives.
>
> Who should be responsible: you, the AI provider, the wallet provider, the merchant, or several of them?
>
> #France2036 #ConsumerProtection #Accountability #AIethics

**In class:** assign two students opposing positions before unveiling the team's recommendations.

## Visual / publication guidance
- Order: Hook → Explain → Debate.
- Captions and illustrations are illustrative content for a class campaign.
- Do not claim these are actual French ministry accounts or endorsements.
- All money shown is fictitious; no online poll results are implied.
